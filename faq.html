🔔 Notifications & Reminders

Questions about reminder timing and refill preparation.

The reminder system is designed to provide advance notice as your expected consumption date approaches. Reminders can be provided 2 days before, 1 day before and on your expected consumption date.
The scheduled reminder times are 12:00 AM, 6:00 AM, 12:00 PM and 6:00 PM according to the user's local time.
The website is designed for users worldwide. Reminder timing should be based on the user's local timezone rather than assuming one specific country or region.
A reminder is simply an opportunity to prepare. You can check your current gas situation and make arrangements for your next refill if necessary.
