🛒 Smart Shopping

Shop smarter.
Waste less.

Turn your meal plan into an organized shopping list, separate what you need from what you already have and keep your weekly cooking preparation easier.

Your meal plan can become your shopping plan

Smart Shopping works directly with your saved Meal Planner. When you create and save your 7-day meal plan, this page detects that saved plan and can turn every meal into an organized shopping list. The list is a planning tool, not an exact recipe or quantity calculation. Always adjust it to your household needs and what you already have at home.

💡 A smarter shopping habit

Before buying everything on your list, check your kitchen first. You may already have some of the ingredients. Using what you already have can help reduce unnecessary purchases and make your meal planning more efficient. Smart Shopping does not know your actual stock or local food prices unless you provide that information, so always review the list before shopping.
