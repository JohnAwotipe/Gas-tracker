🛡️ Gas Safety Center

Safety first.
Always.

Learn important safety habits for using cooking gas at home, recognize warning signs and know when to step away and seek appropriate professional help.

⚠️

Safety comes before saving gas

Never take unnecessary risks to make your gas last longer. If you suspect a gas leak, fire or another serious gas-related hazard, move away from the danger and seek appropriate emergency or professional assistance.

Build safe cooking habits

Safe gas use starts with proper equipment, a suitable cooking environment and attention to unusual signs. The information on this page is intended as general safety guidance and does not replace instructions from your equipment manufacturer or advice from a qualified gas professional.

🛢️

Keep the cylinder in a suitable place

Keep your gas cylinder in an appropriate, stable and well-ventilated location according to the equipment manufacturer's instructions. Keep it away from unnecessary heat sources and areas where it can be knocked over or damaged.

🔥

Pay attention while cooking

Avoid leaving active cooking unattended. Keep combustible items, such as loose paper, cloth and other easily ignited materials, away from open flames and hot surfaces.

🔧

Check your equipment

Pay attention to the condition of your regulator, hose, cooker and connections. If equipment appears damaged, worn or unusual, stop using it and have it assessed by an appropriately qualified professional.

🌬️

Keep the cooking area ventilated

Use your cooking area in a way that provides appropriate ventilation. Never block ventilation openings that are intended to help maintain a safe cooking environment.

👃

Take unusual smells seriously

If you notice an unusual smell that makes you suspect a gas leak, do not ignore it. Move away from the suspected danger and follow appropriate safety and emergency procedures.

👨‍👩‍👧‍👦

Keep children away from gas equipment

Children should not play with cylinders, regulators, hoses, cookers or control knobs. Keep the cooking area appropriately supervised.

🧯

Know your emergency plan

Make sure members of your household know how to move away from danger and how to contact appropriate emergency services or qualified professionals when necessary.

📖

Follow manufacturer instructions

Use your cylinder, regulator, cooker and related equipment according to the instructions supplied with the equipment. When in doubt, ask a qualified professional.

✅ Good safety habits

✓

Keep cooking equipment in appropriate condition.

✓

Pay attention to unusual smells, sounds or visible damage.

✓

Keep flammable materials away from open flames.

✓

Keep children away from cooking equipment.

✓

Use qualified professionals when equipment needs attention.

🚫 Things to avoid

×

Do not ignore a suspected gas leak.

×

Do not use visibly damaged gas equipment.

×

Do not deliberately create flames or sparks around suspected gas leaks.

×

Do not attempt unsafe repairs if you are not qualified.

×

Do not sacrifice safety simply to reduce gas consumption.

🚨 If you suspect a gas leak

If you smell gas or have another reason to suspect a leak, treat the situation seriously. Do not attempt to troubleshoot or repair gas equipment yourself unless you are appropriately qualified.

1

Move away

Move yourself and others away from the suspected danger.

2

Avoid ignition

Avoid flames, sparks and other potential ignition sources near the suspected leak.

3

Get appropriate help

Contact the appropriate emergency service or qualified gas professional for your situation.

4

Stay clear

Do not return to the affected area until it is considered safe by the appropriate person or authority.

👨‍👩‍👧 Make safety a household habit

Everyone who uses the kitchen should understand basic gas safety.

👶

Children

Teach children not to touch cylinders, regulators, hoses or cooker controls without adult supervision.

👨‍🍳

Adults

Adults responsible for cooking should know where the equipment instructions and emergency contacts can be found.

🏠

Everyone

Make sure everyone knows where to move if there is a suspected gas-related emergency.

Need professional help?

Gas equipment problems should be assessed by an appropriately qualified professional. If there is an immediate danger, contact the relevant emergency service for your location.
