Plan your meals around your budget and gas

The planner uses your household size, number of meals, gas preference, selected foods, added foods and special instructions to create a personalized 7-day plan.

A limited budget makes the planner prioritize simpler, affordable meals.
This helps the planner suggest practical portions and batch cooking where it makes sense.
Higher gas-saving preference gives more attention to efficient cooking and shorter cooking sessions.
Tell us anything important: foods you love, foods you dislike, foods you cannot eat, ingredients you already have, your preferred cooking style, how simple you want the meals, or how much you are comfortable spending.
💡 Be specific. Your instructions are treated as meal-planning rules, so the planner can avoid meals that are too expensive or uncomfortable for you.
Add any food you actually eat. Added foods are sent to the planner and are expected to be used in your 7-day plan when practical.
