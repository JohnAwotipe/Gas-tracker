🔥 Gas Usage Tips

To help make your consumption estimate more useful, keep track of how long you spend cooking each meal.

Try not to cook food for longer than the recommended cooking time.

Use medium or low heat whenever possible. Higher heat can use more gas and may cause your gas to finish earlier than your expected date.

Your cooking habits may change from week to week. You may cook more, cook for longer, or have more people eating at home. These changes can affect your actual gas consumption.

Cook smart, save gas, and enjoy your meals! 🍳🔥

Important to Know

Cooking Gas Tracker does not physically measure the gas inside your cylinder. Your expected consumption date is an estimate based on the information you provide and your cooking habits.

You may still have gas remaining when a reminder appears, and your gas may also finish before the estimated date.

Always check your actual cylinder and refill when necessary.
