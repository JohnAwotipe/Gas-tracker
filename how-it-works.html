⚙️ How It Works

Track your gas.
Plan ahead.

Cooking Gas Tracker & Manager helps you keep your gas information organized, track your expected consumption date and prepare for your next refill.

How the system works

The process is simple. You provide information about your gas and your expected consumption date. The tracker stores your information and uses your selected date to help you plan ahead and receive scheduled reminders.

From setup to refill

Here is the basic journey through Cooking Gas Tracker & Manager.

1
📝

Enter your gas information

Start by entering the information requested by the tracker, such as your gas amount, refill information and relevant dates. This gives the tracker the information it needs to organize your gas plan.

2
📅

Set your expected consumption date

Choose the date you expect your current gas supply to be consumed based on your own usage pattern. This date is important because the reminder schedule is based on it.

3
📊

Keep track of your plan

Your tracker gives you a central place to view your gas information, countdown and refill-related details instead of keeping everything in your head.

4
🔔

Receive scheduled reminders

When your expected consumption date gets close, the notification system sends reminders according to the schedule configured for the Cooking Gas Tracker & Manager service.

5
🛒

Prepare for your refill

The purpose of the reminder is to give you time to prepare. You can plan your refill instead of waiting until you suddenly discover that you need more gas.

6
🔄

Update your next cycle

After a refill, update your gas information and expected consumption date so your next tracking cycle can begin.

🔔 How the notifications work

The reminder system is designed to notify you as your expected consumption date approaches.

🟡

2 days before

You receive a preparation reminder telling you to prepare for your cooking gas refill.

🟠

1 day before

The preparation reminder continues so you have another opportunity to plan your refill.

🔴

📌 About the consumption estimate

The expected consumption date is based on the information you provide. Actual gas usage can change because of factors such as the number of people you cook for, meals prepared, cooking time, food type and other cooking habits. You may still have gas remaining when a reminder appears. The notification is intended as a planning reminder, not a guarantee that your cylinder is empty.
