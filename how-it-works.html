Track your cooking gas usage, plan your next refill and receive timely reminders so you can prepare before your gas runs out unexpectedly.

How Reminders Work

Important About Accuracy

Your expected consumption date is based on the information you provide and your normal cooking pattern. Actual cooking gas consumption can vary depending on factors such as the amount of cooking, cooking time, flame level, food type, cookware and household usage. The reminders are intended to help you plan ahead and should not be treated as an exact measurement of the amount of gas remaining in your cylinder.
