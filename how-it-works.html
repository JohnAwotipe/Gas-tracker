🧭 How It Works

Track your gas.
Plan ahead.

Cooking Gas Tracker & Manager helps you record your gas information, set your expected consumption date and receive timely reminders so you can prepare for your next refill before your gas runs out unexpectedly.

Simple from start to finish

Getting started is easy. Enter your cooking gas information, set your expected consumption date and keep your information updated as you go through each refill cycle.

Important about accuracy

Your expected consumption date is based on the information you provide and your normal cooking pattern. Actual gas consumption can change depending on factors such as the number of people cooked for, meals prepared, cooking duration, food type, flame level, cookware and other household habits. The tracker is designed as a planning and awareness tool. It should not be treated as an exact measurement of the amount of gas remaining in your cylinder.
