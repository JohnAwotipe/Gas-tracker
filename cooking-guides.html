Cooking & Usage Guides

Learn how your everyday cooking habits can affect gas usage, understand your household cooking pattern and make better plans for your next refill.

Understand Your Cooking Habits

Every household cooks differently. Your meals, cooking time, household size and cooking habits can all affect how quickly your gas supply is used.

Two households can use the same cylinder size and still consume gas at different rates. This is because gas usage depends on how often and how long the burners are used, as well as what is being cooked.

The goal of these guides is not to give you an exact gas consumption number, but to help you understand your own cooking pattern so you can make better plans.

Simple Cooking Habits

These everyday habits can help you keep your cooking routine organized and make your gas usage easier to understand.

🔥

Use an Appropriate Flame

Choose a flame that suits the cooking task and cookware. Using the highest flame is not automatically necessary for every meal.

🍲

Choose Suitable Cookware

Use cookware that fits the burner and the quantity of food you are preparing. This can make cooking more controlled.

⏱️

Pay Attention to Cooking Time

Notice how long your common meals normally take. This can help you understand your household's normal cooking pattern.

🥘

Plan Your Meals

Knowing what you intend to cook before starting can make the cooking process more organized.

🫕

Use Lids When Suitable

Covering appropriate pots can help retain heat during some cooking tasks. Use lids according to the meal being prepared.

🧅

Prepare Ingredients First

Having your ingredients ready before cooking can help keep the process organized and reduce unnecessary waiting.

Good Cooking Practices

A few simple habits can make your cooking routine easier to manage.

✓ Good Habits

  • Plan what you want to cook before you start.
  • Keep frequently used ingredients organized.
  • Use cookware that is suitable for the burner.
  • Use an appropriate flame for the cooking task.
  • Keep an eye on food while it is cooking.
  • Keep your tracker information updated.

! Things to Avoid

  • Do not leave cooking unattended.
  • Avoid leaving a burner running when it is not needed.
  • Do not ignore unusual gas smells or damaged equipment.
  • Avoid using unsuitable cookware.
  • Do not assume an estimate will always be exact.
  • Never sacrifice cooking safety to save gas.

What Can Affect Gas Usage?

Your consumption can change from one period to another. These are some things worth paying attention to.

1

Household size: the number of people being cooked for can affect cooking frequency and quantity.

2

Number of meals: more frequent cooking can change how quickly gas is used.

3

Cooking duration: longer cooking periods can mean more burner usage.

4

Food type: different meals can require different preparation and cooking times.

5

Flame level: the flame setting used during cooking affects burner usage.

6

Cookware: cookware size, type and condition can affect the cooking process.

Important Reminder

Cooking gas consumption varies from household to household. Estimates are not exact measurements of the amount of gas left in a cylinder. Use the information in these guides together with your own cooking experience and tracker information when planning your next refill.
