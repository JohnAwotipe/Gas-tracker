Cooking & Usage Guides

Learn how everyday cooking habits can affect your gas usage, understand your household cooking pattern and make better decisions when planning your next refill.

Cook Smarter. Understand Your Usage.

Cooking gas consumption is influenced by many things. The way you cook, how long you cook and how often you cook can all affect how quickly your gas supply is used.

There is no single cooking pattern that works for every household. Two homes using the same cylinder size may consume gas at different rates because their meals, cooking frequency, household size and cooking habits are different.

These guides are designed to help you understand those patterns so you can use the information together with the Cooking Gas Tracker when planning your next refill.

Useful Cooking Habits

Small changes in the way you prepare and cook food can make your cooking routine more organized and easier to track.

🔥

Use the Right Flame

Use a flame that suits the size of your cookware and the cooking task. A very high flame is not automatically better or faster for every meal.

🍲

Use Suitable Cookware

Choose cookware that fits the burner and the amount of food you are preparing. Properly sized cookware can make cooking more controlled and convenient.

⏱️

Watch Cooking Time

Pay attention to how long different meals normally take. Understanding your usual cooking time can help you understand your household's gas-use pattern.

🥘

Plan Your Cooking

Organizing meals before you start cooking can help reduce unnecessary delays, repeated heating and forgotten pots.

🫕

Use Lids When Appropriate

Covering suitable pots during cooking can help retain heat and may make some cooking tasks more efficient. Use lids appropriately for the food being prepared.

🧅

Prepare Ingredients First

Have ingredients ready before turning on the burner whenever practical. This keeps the cooking process more organized and reduces unnecessary waiting.

Good Cooking Habits

Use these simple habits as a practical checklist for your everyday cooking routine.

✓ Good Habits to Practice

  • Plan what you want to cook before starting.
  • Keep commonly used ingredients organized and accessible.
  • Use cookware that suits the burner and quantity of food.
  • Use an appropriate flame instead of automatically using the highest setting.
  • Keep an eye on food while it is cooking.
  • Record your gas information regularly when using the tracker.

! Habits to Avoid

  • Do not leave cooking unattended.
  • Avoid keeping a burner running when it is not needed.
  • Do not ignore unusual smells, damaged equipment or other safety concerns.
  • Avoid using cookware that is unsuitable for the burner.
  • Do not assume an estimate will be exactly the same every time you cook.
  • Never compromise basic cooking-gas safety in an attempt to save gas.

Understand Your Cooking Pattern

Your household's cooking pattern can tell you a lot about how your gas supply is being used.

Think about these four areas when trying to understand your normal gas usage.

01

People

How many people are normally cooked for?

02

Meals

How many meals are normally prepared each day?

03

Duration

How long does cooking normally take?

04

Food Type

Which foods do you cook most often?

💡 Why This Matters

A household that cooks several meals every day may use gas differently from a household that cooks less frequently. Understanding your own pattern gives you better information when planning and tracking your gas consumption.

What Can Affect Gas Usage?

Your gas consumption can change from one period to another. These are some of the factors worth paying attention to.

1

Household size: more people may mean more meals and more cooking.

2

Number of meals: cooking frequency affects how often the burner is used.

3

Cooking duration: meals that take longer can result in more burner usage.

4

Food type: different meals can require different preparation and cooking times.

5

Flame level: the flame setting used during cooking can affect how gas is consumed.

6

Cookware: the type, size and condition of cookware can affect the cooking process.

Important Reminder

Gas usage estimates are not exact measurements. Actual consumption can vary because cooking habits and conditions change. Use the Cooking Gas Tracker as a planning and awareness tool, and always prioritize safe cooking practices.
