Every household cooks differently

The amount of gas a household uses can change depending on the number of people being cooked for, the meals prepared, cooking time, cooking habits and other everyday factors. Understanding your own pattern can make it easier to plan your next refill.

01
🍲

Plan your meals

Knowing what you intend to cook can help you organize your ingredients, cookware and cooking time before you start.

02
🔥

Use an appropriate flame

Choose a flame that suits the cooking task and cookware. Using the highest flame is not automatically necessary for every meal.

03
🍳

Choose suitable cookware

Use cookware that fits the burner and the amount of food you are preparing. Suitable cookware can make cooking more controlled.

04
🫕

Use lids when appropriate

For meals where it is suitable, using a lid can help retain heat and may reduce unnecessary cooking time.

05
🧅

Prepare ingredients first

Having your ingredients ready before cooking can help you avoid unnecessary delays while the burner is running.

06
⏱️

Pay attention to cooking time

Notice which meals normally take longer to prepare. Your usual cooking times can help you understand your household's cooking pattern.

07
🥘

Understand your food choices

Different meals can require different preparation methods and cooking times. Notice the foods your household prepares most often.

08
📊

Track your usage

Keep your gas information and refill dates updated so you can start recognizing your household's normal pattern over time.

09
👨‍👩‍👧‍👦

Consider household size

The number of people being cooked for can affect how much food is prepared and how frequently cooking takes place.

10
📅

Learn your household pattern

After several cooking cycles, your own records can become a useful reference when planning future refills.

What can affect gas usage?

Your consumption can change from one period to another. These are some things worth paying attention to.

👨‍👩‍👧‍👦

Household size

The number of people being cooked for can affect the amount and frequency of cooking.

🍽️

Number of meals

Cooking more frequently can change how quickly gas is used.

⏱️

Cooking duration

Longer cooking periods can mean more burner usage.

🥘

Food type

Different meals can require different preparation and cooking times.

🔥

Flame level

The flame setting used during cooking affects burner usage.

🍳

Cookware

Cookware size, type and condition can affect the cooking process.

💡 Things to remember

Your cooking routine does not have to be exactly the same every day. The goal is to understand your normal pattern.

Your routine matters Your own cooking history can provide useful information for future planning.
Usage can change A change in meals, household activity or cooking frequency can change your gas usage.
Estimates are not exact Use the tracker as a planning and awareness tool rather than an exact measurement of gas remaining.

🚨 Safety comes first

Gas-saving habits should never replace basic cooking-gas safety. Do not ignore unusual smells, damaged equipment or other potential safety problems. If you suspect a gas leak or another serious safety issue, move away from the danger area and follow appropriate safety procedures or contact qualified help.
