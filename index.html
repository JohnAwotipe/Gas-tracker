⚠️ NOTICE / CAUTION

To meet up with the consumption date, you must take note of your cooking time for every meal for gas management. Your food must not exceed the normal timing of a particular food. And remember to cook on medium-low heat unless if preferred. But remember the higher the heat, the more consumption of gas which might affect your expected consumption date. Also take note that how you cook in the first week will be different from how you cook in the second week which also affect the accuracy of your consumption date. We wish you happy cooking.
